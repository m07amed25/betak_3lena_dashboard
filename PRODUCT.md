# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Support Agents, Moderators, Accountants, Operations Managers, and Super Admins. They use this in a professional work context to manage the Betak-Alena home-services marketplace.

## Product Purpose

An internal operations dashboard used by staff to run the platform, manage requests, handle KYC/approvals, provide technical support, and oversee accounting and payments. Success means staff can perform these tasks efficiently without confusion.

## Positioning

The central nervous system for Betak-Alena operations, turning complex marketplace data into clear, actionable workflows for staff.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, modern, approachable.

## Anti-references

No sketchy SVGs or playful themes. No generic SaaS "cream" templates. No cluttered, outdated enterprise tables.

## Design Principles

- **Clarity over density:** Provide information progressively rather than overwhelming the user.
- **Workflow-driven:** Actions and data should be structured around the staff's daily tasks.
- **Accessible and approachable:** Maintain a friendly, modern feel without sacrificing professional efficiency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard best practices (proper contrast, semantic HTML, keyboard navigation). RTL (Arabic) and LTR (English) bidirectional support is mandatory.
