#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The design feels entirely like an unedited shadcn template. The presence of "Acme Inc", Apple/Google login, and a "Sign up" link are dead giveaways of "AI slop" or template copy-pasting for an internal admin dashboard where users do not self-register and Apple SSO has no business justification.

**Deterministic scan**: The CLI scanner returned 0 explicit pattern violations, but manual source inspection caught severe business-logic anti-patterns.

**Visual overlays**: No reliable user-visible overlay is available (no browser injection).

#### Overall Impression
The foundation is solid and clean (standard shadcn Card), but it fails to align with the product's actual use case. It's built like a B2C SaaS app, not an internal marketplace control center.

#### What's Working
1. **Clean Layout**: The centered card on a muted background is perfectly appropriate for an admin login.
2. **Clear Typography**: Good hierarchy with "Welcome back" as the display text.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P0] Remove Self-Registration (Sign up)**
  - **Why it matters**: Dashboard users are operations, support, and finance staff created by a Super Admin. They cannot self-register.
  - **Fix**: Delete the "Don't have an account? Sign up" section.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill`

- **[P1] Remove Unauthorized Social Logins**
  - **Why it matters**: Apple SSO has no business justification here. If Google is used, it must be restricted to the company domain. Having random consumer SSO buttons on an admin panel is a security anti-pattern and confusing.
  - **Fix**: Remove the Apple and Mobile auth buttons. Keep Google only if Workspace SSO is intended, otherwise remove all social providers and use email/password + TOTP.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill`

- **[P3] Fix Boilerplate Branding**
  - **Why it matters**: "Acme Inc" destroys trust and feels unfinished.
  - **Fix**: Update the copy to "Betak-Alena".
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable clarify`

#### Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User / Admin)**: Expects a secure, no-nonsense login. Seeing consumer Apple SSO and "Sign up" makes the system feel insecure and consumer-grade, not like an enterprise control center.
**Jordan (First-Timer / New Employee)**: Might click "Sign up" thinking they need to create their account, rather than waiting for their admin invite, leading to confusion and support tickets.

#### Minor Observations
- No visible loading state for the submit button during authentication.
- "Terms of Service" and "Privacy Policy" links are likely irrelevant for an internal tool; an "Internal Usage Policy" might be better.

#### Questions to Consider
- Is Google Workspace SSO actually required, or should we strip it down to pure email/password credentials for now?
- Should we add a mention of the mandatory 2FA/TOTP requirement noted in the product spec?
